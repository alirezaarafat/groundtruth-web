Three steps,
no dead ends.

Most reporting tools stop at "submitted." GroundTruth keeps going until the issue is actually resolved.

1

Report what you see

Title, a short description, a photo, and the exact spot on the map. Takes under a minute.

2

It gets verified

An authority for that area checks it's genuine — not a duplicate, not spam — before it moves forward.

3

Tracked to resolved

Status updates, a resolution note, and a before/after photo once it's actually fixed.
